- narrow-band DG level-set method: level set primarily defined on the cells intersected by the interface
- extension of the level set to the face neighbors of the cut cells (all face neighbors or only the downstream ones): global face-based ghost-penalty extension or closest-point extension
- alternatively: advected band of the cut cells and several layers of vertex neighbors with geometric redistancing of the layers
- alternatively: semi-Lagrangian band, where the level set is only stored on the cut cells and evaluated on the cut cells and their vertex neighbors by back-tracing the characteristics (no extension)
- explicit upwind DG advection (non-conservative form) on the narrow band with a prescribed velocity, optionally with a bound-preserving limiter (local maximum principle or sign preservation) or an artificial viscosity in the cells flagged by a modal smoothness indicator
- elliptic reinitialization on the cut cells (SIPG coupling across all faces between cut cells or only across the faces crossed by the interface), with the interface penalty term on the zero level set of the advected level set or at advected interface points (front tracking with seeding in gaps, merging of points and merge and break-up rules for topology changes, which remove the points of colliding parts of the interface (merging, pinch-off); removes the shrinkage of convex interfaces by the linear interpolation of the semi-Lagrangian band for p = 1; optionally, the cut cells around unresolved points, e.g., thin filaments, are redistanced at the subcell scale from the points and projected onto the cell polynomials, which either replaces the reinitialized level set or is prescribed in the reinitialization of the other cut cells)
- optional continuous level set on the cut cells (constrained L2-projection of the DG level set with an interface penalty) with normal vector and curvature for the output and as input for surface tension
- restart (subsection `restart`): with `save` >= 0, the state of the simulation (mesh, cut cells, level set on the cut cells and on the band, signs of the cells, reference of the sub-cycling, advected interface points, time and output records) is written every `write time step size` (wall-clock seconds or simulation time, `time type`), after the last time step and, if the `max wall time` is exceeded, before the simulation stops; `load` = i continues from the state i (0: latest, older states are kept up to `save` - 1) and reproduces the uninterrupted simulation for the same number of processes
- cases: `rotating_circle`, `vortex_bubble` (single vortex in 2D, deformation field of LeVeque in 3D), `zalesak_disk` (rigid-body rotation of Zalesak's slotted disk), `static_circle` (reinitialization of a scaled signed distance function of a circle/sphere at rest for convergence studies) `merging_circles` (two circles/spheres, which move towards each other with u = -U sign(x - m_x) e_x and merge; exact solution: union of the two translated disks/balls, with the gap, the neck, the area and the length of the interface), `breakup_circles` (two overlapping circles, which are squeezed towards their axis with u = -U sign(y - m_y) e_y, such that the neck pinches off at t = sqrt(r^2 - D^2/4) / U; exact solution: {|y - m_y| + U t < h_0(x)} with the half-height h_0 of the initial union, two lenses after the pinch-off, with the gap, the neck, the area and the length of the interface; 2D) and `static_particles` (particles from a particle list at rest, e.g., for the reinitialization of close interfaces; optionally with a cell-wise initial level set, which is the signed distance of the particle intersecting each cut cell)
